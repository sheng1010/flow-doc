Privacy Policy — Flow Doc for Salesforce

Last updated: 2026-11-04

  • The extension reads your existing Salesforce session cookie (sid) for *.my.salesforce.com only to call the Salesforce API from your browser on your behalf.
  • It reads flow definitions and flow metadata (names, types, trigger objects, versions, last-modified user name and date, and the flow's steps, conditions, fields and variables). It does not read business records.
  • All processing happens locally in your browser. Nothing is sent to the developer or any third party. Nothing is stored after you close the page.
  • Files you export (CSV, Excel, Markdown) are saved to your computer and are under your control.
  • Optional feedback: only if you click Send, your message (and email if you type it) and the extension version are sent to our form provider Formspree.
  • No analytics, no ads, no sale of data. Read-only: the extension never changes your org.
